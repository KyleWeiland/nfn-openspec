The Theranos Playbook Is Quietly Returning in Cybersecurity

April 4, 2026 · via govinfosecurity.com

Market Pressures Are Rewarding Storytelling More Than Validation, Operational Value. When health tech company Theranos collapsed, much of the attention focused on the personality of its founder Elizabeth Holmes and the scale of the deception.

Theranos emerged in an ecosystem where technological ambition, investor pressure and persuasive storytelling reinforced one another. That dynamic should feel uncomfortably familiar to anyone watching today's cybersecurity vendor landscape.

None of this suggests that cybersecurity vendors are committing fraud on the scale of Theranos. But the structural incentives share a troubling resemblance: markets that prioritize growth narratives over demonstrated outcomes, investors who lack the technical depth to evaluate claims, and buyers overwhelmed by complexity who default to brand recognition.

Markets that reward storytelling alone eventually produce something else.
